# Decision: stylized characters on real rock, and buy what saves a week

*2026-08-22. Two of `DIRTBAG.md` §11's four open questions, answered at last —
they had been sitting unanswered since the concept doc was written, and the
Early Access decision surfaced them because assets cannot be chosen without
them.*

## The decisions

**Art direction: stylized characters, realistic rock.** The concept doc's
own recommendation, taken.

**Budget: whatever saves a build week.** Paid packs are preferred over
kitbashing wherever they remove real time.

## Why this one is right and not just cheaper

`DIRTBAG.md` §9 names the project's top risk as *"session isn't
tense/legible in 3D"*, and the second-order version of that risk is the
uncanny valley of climbing: **a realistic human climbing badly looks
broken, and a stylized one climbing the same way looks fine.** This project
has already decided it will not simulate climbing — CLAUDE.md's core call is
*"no physical climbing simulation, no hand-IK"* — so the character will be
playing back a small set of authored poses on a spline for the whole game.

Realistic characters would spend the entire budget fighting that decision.
Stylized ones are in on it.

Meanwhile the **rock has to be real**, because it is the thing the game is
about and the thing the camera looks at. A stylized boulder is a shape; a
real one has the features a route description is describing. The shade line
moving across actual rock is the pitch in §1 of the concept doc.

The combination is also, usefully, not a look anybody else has.

## What it rules out

- **MetaHuman.** Not for the player, not for the Lot.
- **Fully stylized rock.** Synty-style valleys are cheaper and faster and
  would make this a different game.

## What it does not decide

`DIRTBAG.md` §11's other two are still open and are **not** blocked by
anything: whether the 2D game's canon carries over, and PC-first. Neither
gates an asset purchase, so neither is urgent.

First versus third person is *also* still formally open, but it is being
answered by construction rather than by argument: the session camera is a
watched third-person shot that follows the climber, tightens on a crux and
breathes with the pump (`notes/phase5-the-session-reads.md`). Walking around
is third person on Epic's template. **It is third person.** Written down
here so it stops being listed as open.
