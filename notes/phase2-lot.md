# The Lot — placing it (Evan)

The people are built and wired. This is placement only, and it is small:
**one actor.** Everything else is keys at walls you already have.

---

## 1. Rebuild and meet them

Pull, delete `Binaries`, reopen the `.uproject`, **Yes** to rebuild.

The cast, for this world:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Margo** | V5 | twenty seasons here, knows every line and undersells all of them |
| **Dev** | V8 | young, strong, here for the hard stuff — will have your project if you leave it lying around |
| **Trish** | V3 | psyched beyond all reason, gives terrible beta with total confidence |
| **Ray** | — | hasn't pulled on in years, put up half the crag, tells you which half |
| **Bo** | — | passing through for the last four months, fixes vans for beer |

They climb whether you do or not. Their strength on any given day comes from
the world seed and the date, so it is the same every time you load — but
what they have *taken* is yours to lose.

## 2. Place the fire (the one actor)

**Place Actors** → `Dirtbag Day Spot` → drop it near the van or the pads.
Set **Kind = `Fire`**.

That is it. Leave **Wait For Window** ticked and one press sits exactly
until the rock comes good, same as the Rest spot — but with people, which
is the entire difference:

> `Sit at the fire?  (E)  -  Margo, Dev, Trish, Ray, Bo.  The rock comes good in 95 minutes.`

and afterwards something somebody is on this week:

> `1.6 hours at the fire.  Dev has been on the low traverse into Chalk Ghost all week.`

**Keep it clear of the walls' triggers**, as with every other spot.

You can keep a plain `Rest` spot too. Sitting alone and sitting at the fire
cost the same hours; the fire also buys rapport, and rapport is what beta
and psyche are priced on. If you find you never use the plain Rest spot,
that is a useful answer and I will fold it in.

## 3. Ask for beta — **B** at any crag wall

No placement. Walk up to a line and press **B**:

> `Margo walks you through it.`

Whoever can actually help most answers, which is not always whoever you know
best — Trish is delighted to help and cannot. On an unclimbed line you get
the honest answer:

> `Nobody here has done it.`

Rapport decides how much they hand over: a stranger tells you where the crux
is, a friend gives you most of what they know. Nobody ever gives you all of
it.

## 4. Losing a line

The sharp end of the whole thing. When you sleep, the Lot's day resolves
too, and if somebody took an open line you wake to it on the HUD in orange:

> `Dev got the arete left of Diesel.`

Measured with no player in the world: **nothing goes in the first 30 days**,
both moderates by day 90, four of five within a year, and the V10 blank wall
not for three. So there is a month of grace and then real pressure, and the
long-term prize is safe because nobody is strong enough for it yet.

If it happens to you, the line is gone. It is somebody's route now, with
somebody's name on it.

## 5. What to send back

1. **Does the fire make waiting worth it?** This is the question the Lot
   exists to answer. Sitting alone and sitting with people cost the same
   hours — if the fire does not feel different, the fix is that people need
   to *do* more than talk, and I would rather know that now.
2. **Is losing a line dreadful in a good way or a bad one?** A month of
   grace is a guess. If Dev taking the arete feels cheap, the chance comes
   down; if nobody ever takes anything, it goes up.
3. **Is Trish funny or annoying?** She is meant to be both. Her beta is
   genuinely worthless — she cannot climb the lines you are asking about —
   and the game does not tell you that except by her never being the one who
   answers.
4. **Does the news land in the morning?** It stays up all day on purpose.
