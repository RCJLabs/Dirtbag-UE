# Phase 3 at the desk (Evan)

Two actors to place. Everything else is already wired.

## 1. The van spot — `Dirtbag Day Spot`, **Kind = `Van`**

Put it at the van. Walk up:

> `Sort the van? (E) - the belt is on borrowed time.  $210`

It does the best repair you can actually afford: **replace** properly if the
money is there, **patch** if not, and **bodge** if there is nothing at all.
Bodging is free, costs four hours, and will not hold — which is the promise
that being broke is a bad week rather than a dead save.

## 2. The gear shop — `Dirtbag Day Spot`, **Kind = `GearShop`**

Anywhere; the town block does not exist yet, so treat it as the shop in the
next valley.

> `Shoes? (E) - the rubber is going; worth a resole.  $210`

Resole at $55 while the uppers hold, $165 for new when they will not. Two
resoles to a pair.

## What changed without placement

- **Driving wears the van.** Every Travel spot now drives: hours on the
  clock, hours on the van, and a chance the thing you have been ignoring
  picks this morning. If something goes, the drive still happens and the
  van is dead when you arrive.
- **A broken van refuses to drive.** The only "no" in the game. The way out
  is four hours, not money.
- **Shoes wear by the move**, faster the harder you pull, and dead rubber
  costs grades on edging holds long before it stops you — a worn pair
  quietly pushes you onto slopers.
- **Bills you cannot pay become debt.** Cash floors at zero and the
  shortfall waits; the next wage clears it before it reaches your pocket.
  The HUD shows `owe $85`.
- **The HUD** carries the van's state and what you owe.

## What to judge

1. **Is a breakdown dramatic or annoying?** Two or three in the first year,
   climbing to about nine a year as the van ages. If it reads as noise, the
   fix is fewer and more expensive.
2. **Does bodging feel like a lifeline or a cheat?** It has to be genuinely
   available or being broke ends the save, but it should sting.
3. **Do you ever notice the shoes?** The probe never climbed on dead rubber
   because it always resoled in time. A player short of cash will meet it.
4. **Does debt read as pressure or as a nuisance?**
